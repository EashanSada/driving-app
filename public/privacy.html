Summary: RadianDrive is dedicated to promoting safe driving practices and simplifying Graduated Driver Licensing (GDL) logbooks. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not track you across third-party websites or services for advertising.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the RadianDrive mobile application and related web services (collectively, the "Application" or "Service"), developed under the DriveSafe Youth Initiative. This document outlines how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when using our app, in strict compliance with the Apple App Store Review Guidelines (Guideline 5.1).

2. Information We Collect

To provide telematics, trip logging, and driving safety analytics, RadianDrive collects the following categories of data:

A. Precise Location Data (GPS)

  • What we collect: Latitude, longitude, altitude, bearing, instantaneous speed, and accuracy timestamps.
  • When we collect it: While an active driving trip is in progress (including background execution if enabled so that your trip continues logging when your screen is locked or you switch apps).
  • Purpose: To record trip distance, calculate speed limit adherence, analyze route conditions, compute turn radius kinematics, and maintain your supervised driving logbook.

B. Motion and Sensor Data

  • What we collect: Accelerometer and gyroscope data from your device's Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU).
  • Purpose: To detect critical driving safety events, such as harsh braking, sudden acceleration, and high-speed cornering, enabling the app to produce an objective safety score.

C. Account and Profile Information

  • What we collect: Email address, user display name, and securely hashed authentication credentials when you create an account via Supabase Auth.
  • Optional profile details: Target practice driving hours, licensing permit state/jurisdiction, and supervision status.

D. Trip & Telematics Records

  • What we collect: Trip start/finish timestamps, total mileage, duration, day vs. night driving hours (for graduated licensing compliance), weather conditions, and aggregate safety ratings.

E. Device and Technical Diagnostics

  • What we collect: Device model, operating system version, app version, and standard non-identifying crash/diagnostic logs for bug fixes and stability improvements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for legitimate application functionality:

  • Calculating real-time and post-drive safety scores and driver coaching feedback.
  • Automating official graduated driver licensing (GDL) hours verification (e.g., separating daylight and nighttime driving hours required for road tests).
  • Synchronizing your driving logbooks across your authorized devices via our encrypted database backend.
  • Diagnosing crashes, enhancing app performance, and releasing software updates.

4. How We Share Information and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal or driving data to third-party data brokers, insurers, or marketing networks.

We only share data with essential infrastructure service providers that adhere to rigorous security standards:

  • Supabase: Provides encrypted database hosting and authentication services. Data is stored in secure cloud data centers with Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Apple Inc.: Processes anonymized crash analytics and diagnostic logs if you have opted in at the iOS system level.
  • Parent / Guardian / Supervisor (User-Initiated Only): If you deliberately choose to share or export your certified GDL logbook, reports are shared only with the individuals you authorize.

6. Data Storage, Security, and Retention

We employ industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to secure your personal information. All network communication between the RadianDrive app and our servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Database records are isolated per user using database-level access rules (Supabase RLS).

Your data is retained for as long as your account remains active to preserve your official driving log history.

7. Account and Data Deletion (Your Rights)

In accordance with Apple App Store Guideline 5.1.1(v), you have full control over your personal data:

  • In-App Deletion: You may request the deletion of individual trips or your entire account directly from within the Application settings.
  • Email Request: You can request immediate and permanent deletion of your account, driving logs, and all associated personal data by emailing us at support@radiandrive.app (or contacting the project administrator). Upon request, all personal data is permanently deleted from our primary databases within 30 days.

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

RadianDrive is designed for learner drivers preparing for their driver’s licenses, typically aged 15 and older, under parent or guardian supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us immediately, and we will delete such information promptly.
