# Privacy Policy for RadianDrive

**Application:** RadianDrive (DriveSafe Youth Initiative)  
**Bundle Identifier:** `com.radiandrive.app`  
**Effective Date:** September 27, 2026  
**Contact:** support@radiandrive.app  

---

## 1. Summary
RadianDrive is a youth driver safety platform designed to assist learner drivers and families with real-time driving telematics, Graduated Driver Licensing (GDL) practice hour tracking, and objective safety scoring. **We do not sell your personal data, and we do not track you across other companies' apps or websites for targeted advertising.**

---

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Precise Location Data (GPS)
- **Data Collected:** Latitude, longitude, altitude, heading, instantaneous speed, and accuracy timestamps.
- **When:** Collected during active driving trip recording sessions. If background location is granted, location coordinates are recorded while the app is minimized or the screen is locked to ensure complete trip records.
- **Purpose:** To log trip routes, calculate speed limit compliance, evaluate turn radius kinematics, calculate total miles driven, and satisfy official graduated licensing logbook requirements.

### B. Motion & Sensor Data
- **Data Collected:** Accelerometer and gyroscope readings from the device's hardware Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU).
- **Purpose:** To detect safety-critical events such as harsh braking, sudden acceleration, and sharp turns, generating an objective driving safety score.

### C. Account & Profile Information
- **Data Collected:** Email address, display name, and securely hashed passwords managed through Supabase Authentication.
- **Purpose:** To identify the user, authenticate secure sessions, and synchronize driving history across devices.

### D. Driving Telematics & GDL Records
- **Data Collected:** Trip start/end times, trip durations, daylight vs. nighttime driving hour classifications, weather conditions, and safety event counts.
- **Purpose:** To provide teen drivers and supervising parents/guardians with accurate state-compliant GDL log reports for DMV licensing tests.

### E. Diagnostics & Technical Logs
- **Data Collected:** Device model, iOS operating system version, app version, and crash diagnostic data.
- **Purpose:** To troubleshoot bugs, ensure native iOS sensor stability, and improve overall app reliability.

---

## 3. How We Use Information
We use data solely to provide and improve the RadianDrive experience:
- Calculating telematics safety scores and driver coaching feedback.
- Automating graduated driver licensing (GDL) logbook completion.
- Storing and retrieving your driving sessions across devices.
- Maintaining app performance and security.

---

## 4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing
We **never** sell, rent, or trade your data to third-party data brokers, insurers, or marketing companies. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers necessary to run the app:
- **Supabase Inc.:** Cloud database and user authentication service. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and protected with Row-Level Security (RLS).
- **Apple Inc.:** Collects anonymized crash logs and system metrics if enabled by the user in iOS Settings.

## 6. Data Retention and Account Deletion (Apple Guideline 5.1.1(v))
- **Retention:** Driving logs and account data are retained while the user account is active.
- **Deletion:** Users have the right to request deletion of their account and all associated driving telemetry data at any time.
  - To request account deletion, email **support@radiandrive.app** with your registered account email. All associated data will be permanently wiped within 30 days.

---

## 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K)
RadianDrive is designed for learner drivers preparing for their driver’s licenses (typically ages 15+) under parent/guardian supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information, please contact us for immediate deletion.
